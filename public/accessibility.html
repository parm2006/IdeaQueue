Accessibility statement

Updated October 20, 2026

IdeaQueue aims to make capturing and reviewing ideas usable for people with disabilities. This page describes available controls, current limits, and how to report a barrier.

Accessibility status

Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA is the reference for accessibility improvements. A complete evaluation of the app against these guidelines has not been completed, so this statement does not claim full or partial conformance. Publishing a statement does not establish that the app is accessible.

Using the app

  • Use Tab and Shift + Tab to move between controls. Use Enter or Space to activate buttons.
  • When the notebook has focus, use the up/down arrows or Page Up/Page Down to browse notes. Home returns to the first note. Long notes may scroll before turning to another note.
  • Use Ctrl + K on Windows or ⌘ + K on Mac to open Add idea. You can also use the Add idea button.
  • Type notes without using dictation. Voice capture depends on your browser and its speech service.
  • On a bucket control, use the Menu key or Shift + F10 to open its menu.
  • These information pages use headings, visible link focus, a skip link, and text that reflows with the window. Your browser’s zoom and text-size settings can enlarge them.

Current limits

Screen-reader use, keyboard focus across every dialog, high zoom, contrast across all note colors, and the complete sign-in and mobile flows still need a full accessibility evaluation. Notebook page changes and gesture-based navigation may be difficult with some assistive technologies. These are areas requiring verification, rather than a complete list of barriers.

The app requires JavaScript and a current browser. Google sign-in, browser speech services, and iPhone Shortcuts have their own interfaces and accessibility behavior. These information pages can be read without JavaScript or signing in.

Report a barrier or request help

Contact the maintainer through the IdeaQueue repository. Describe the page, what you were trying to do, what happened, and your browser or assistive technology if you wish. You do not need to disclose a disability.

GitHub is currently the published contact route and may require an account to submit an issue. A dedicated private, account-free contact method is not yet published. Do not include note contents, passwords, tokens, or other sensitive information in a public issue; ask the maintainer to arrange a private channel.

This statement will be updated as barriers are verified and addressed.
