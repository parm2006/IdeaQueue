Privacy policy

Updated October 20, 2026

IdeaQueue stores your ideas so you can capture, organize, and review them across devices. This notice explains what the app collects, how it uses that information, and when information leaves the app.

Information we store

We store your account identifier and sign-in information supplied by your identity provider, which may include your email address, name, and profile image. We store the ideas and context you submit, their bucket, priority, color, creation and review times, and account preferences such as cleanup and deleted buckets.

For integrations, we store token hashes, token labels, owner identifiers, creation and last-use times, and revocation status. Raw integration tokens are displayed when created; keep your copies secret. Your browser stores sign-in session information. Older browser notes may remain in local storage. Exports are saved to your device; the iPhone Shortcut can keep an offline outbox on your phone.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate you, save and sync notes, order your queue, apply your preferences, operate integrations you choose, troubleshoot failures, and protect the service. Hosting and authentication providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser information, and operational logs when delivering these services.

Who receives information

The standard app uses Supabase for authentication and database storage, Google for Google sign-in, and Vercel for hosting and API delivery. A Sites-hosted version uses its hosting and sign-in services instead. See the Supabase privacy policy, Google privacy policy, and Vercel privacy policy for their practices. Authorized service operators may access information as needed to maintain, secure, or support the service. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or to respond to valid legal process.

The current app does not include advertising or a data-sale feature. Your cards are not a public feed. Account access controls restrict ordinary users to their own notes; this is not end-to-end encrypted storage, and no online service can guarantee absolute security.

AI agents and your choices

Agent connections are available only in the Supabase/Vercel app, not the Sites-hosted version. Ending a card with AI or agent does not by itself send it to an agent: matching card data is sent when a connected client reads it.

Connector client registration requires IQ sign-in and belongs to that account; public registration is disabled. When you connect an agent, sign in to the same IQ account and approve the account connection once. You do not approve individual cards. The connector can read up to 100 of your queued cards whose final separate word is AI or agent, in any capitalization, including their text and context. All other cards and reviewed cards are excluded. Removing that final word stops future sharing of that card. Existing cards with those endings are included once you connect an agent.

Agents receive limited connector credentials, not your website login session or database credentials. OAuth uses short-lived access tokens and rotating refresh tokens; advanced clients may use a manually created token instead. Anyone holding a valid connector token can read the matching cards until it expires or is revoked. You can revoke a connection in Settings.

Agent access is read-only. It does not automatically launch an agent. Unsharing a card or revoking its agent token stops future reads; it cannot erase copies already received. Your chosen agent provider may retain or process received information under its own policy. A capture token is separate: it can create notes, but cannot read existing notes.

Dictation, searches, and links

If you start dictation, your browser or operating system speech service processes microphone audio under its own policy. IdeaQueue saves the resulting text you submit, not an audio recording. Clicking “Find out” sends the displayed idea text to Google as a search query. Opening external links, installing a Shortcut, or sharing an export sends information to the destination you choose.

Browser storage and tracking

The app uses browser storage to keep your sign-in session and any legacy local notes. The current app code does not include advertising analytics or a mechanism that changes behavior in response to Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signals. Hosting and identity providers may independently process requests under their policies.

Retention and deletion

Connector credentials unused for 14 days stop working and are removed by hourly cleanup, along with their token history. Unused client registrations and account connector counters are also removed after 14 days; expired authorization codes and unusable or revoked credentials may be removed sooner. Successful connector requests or refreshes count as activity. This cleanup does not delete notes or accounts. Account-level connector usage counters enforce hourly and daily limits; ordinary website use does not count.

Queued notes remain until removed through the service or an account deletion request. On the Supabase app, reviewed notes are eligible for scheduled hourly removal by default; turning hourly cleanup off retains each reviewed note for seven days after review. Actual removal depends on the cleanup job running. Provider logs and backups may follow separate retention schedules; removal from the active app does not promise immediate removal from every backup or a connected agent.

You can view and correct your notes, export them in Settings, remove AI/agent suffixes, and revoke integration tokens. Signing out does not delete your account. Device exports, legacy local storage, and the Shortcut outbox must be removed on your own devices.

Privacy requests and applicable rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent where consent applies, or complain to your local data protection authority. For account deletion or another privacy request, contact the project maintainer through the IdeaQueue repository to arrange a private channel. Do not post notes, tokens, or sensitive account details in a public issue. We may need to verify account ownership before acting on a request.

Children, processing locations, and changes

IdeaQueue is not intended for children under 13. Contact the maintainer if you believe a child has provided personal information. Service providers may process information outside your country under their applicable terms. Changes to these practices will be reflected on this page with a revised date. Check this notice before using new integrations or sharing sensitive information.
