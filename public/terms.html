Terms of use

Updated October 20, 2026

These terms describe use of IdeaQueue, a tool for capturing, organizing, and reviewing ideas. Read them with the privacy policy, which explains data handling, and the accessibility statement, which describes controls and current limits.

Your account and use

Use an account you are authorized to access. Keep your sign-in account and integration tokens secure. Do not attempt to access another person’s notes, bypass access controls, disrupt the service, or upload content you have no right to use. Follow applicable law when using the service or sharing information about other people.

IdeaQueue is not intended for children under 13. If local law requires a higher minimum age or parental authorization, those requirements also apply.

Your notes

You retain your rights in the content you submit. You allow the service to store, process, and transmit that content as needed to provide the features you use, including integrations you enable. This permission does not transfer ownership of your notes.

You are responsible for the content you submit and the destinations you choose to share it with. Avoid storing passwords, access tokens, or information whose exposure or loss would cause serious harm.

Agents and external services

A connected agent with your agent token can read selected queued cards and their context. Automatic visibility includes a card whose final separate word is AI or agent, in any capitalization, unless you explicitly set it to Private. Review existing cards before connecting a client. Agent connections are read-only and do not automatically execute tasks.

Revoking a token or changing visibility stops future access through that token or selection; it cannot recall information already copied. Capture tokens have a separate purpose and can create notes without reading existing notes. External agents, Google search, speech services, and other destinations operate under their own terms and policies. Check what you share and review any agent output before relying on it.

Retention and copies

Reviewed notes are subject to automatic cleanup. In the Supabase app, hourly cleanup is enabled by default; when disabled, each reviewed note is retained for seven days after review, then removed when the cleanup job runs. Restore a reviewed note to keep it in the queue. Export important notes using Settings and keep your own backup.

Signing out does not delete your account. For deletion requests, use the contact route below. The privacy policy explains limits involving provider backups, device copies, and information already shared with agents.

Availability and changes

The service may have errors, interruptions, or changes. There is no guarantee of uninterrupted availability, recovery of lost notes, or compatibility with every browser or integration. Do not rely on IdeaQueue as your only copy of important information or for emergency use.

These terms may be revised as the service changes, with the updated date shown above. Applicable legal rights, including rights that cannot be excluded by agreement, continue to apply.

Questions and requests

The published contact route is the project maintainer through the IdeaQueue repository. For a privacy request or account issue, ask to arrange a private channel. Do not post personal notes, credentials, or tokens in a public issue.
